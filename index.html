What is Exisdios?

Exisdios is a philosophy built around compassion, understanding and the recognition of existence.

It explores how we understand ourselves, one another and the world around us, and the responsibility we have to live with kindness and consideration.

To prevent as much harm as reasonably possible, and to treat existence with dignity, kindness and understanding.

An Open Philosophy

Exisdios is not intended to be imposed upon anyone. There is room for disagreement, uncertainty and different interpretations.

Its ideas are open to examination and development. You do not need to have all the answers to explore Exisdios.
