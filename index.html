What is Exisdios?

Exisdios is a personal philosophy and spiritual understanding centered on compassion, dignity, reason, and personal responsibility. It is not an institution, a dogmatic creed, or a commercial enterprise. It is a living, evolving personal journal documenting my observations on existence, ethics, and the quiet spiritual presence that accompanies my journey.

This space exists to share these thoughts openly—not to impose them upon anyone, nor to declare universal dogma, but to offer a quiet retreat for honest reflection, intellectual humility, and moral purpose.

Ethical & Supporting Foundations

The Six Pillars of Exisdios

Principles for everyday life, guiding our relationship with existence, reason, and one another.

Core Ethical Mandate

"To prevent as much harm as reasonably possible within your control with kidness and dignity for all."

Ⅰ. Ethical Pillars

1. Dignity

Ethical

Every conscious being possesses intrinsic worth. Dignity requires that we never reduce human beings, animals, or living creatures to mere instruments for our own convenience or satisfaction.

Application in Daily Life:
  • Treating individuals with fundamental respect regardless of status, opinion, or background.
  • Honoring self-worth and refusing to tolerate humiliating conditions for oneself or others.
  • Acknowledging the quiet dignity of non-human creatures and the natural ecosystems they inhabit.

2. Kindness

Ethical

Kindness is the active extension of goodwill into the world. It is not passive politeness, but an intentional choice to ease the burdens carried by others whenever opportunity arises.

Application in Daily Life:
  • Offering patient listening and gentle responses in moments of friction.
  • Performing quiet acts of assistance without expecting recognition or return.
  • Cultivating warmth toward oneself when facing failure or personal shortcoming.

3. Prevention of Harm

Ethical

The primary moral obligation of Exisdios is the active minimization of suffering. Where we hold power or influence, we carry the duty to protect vulnerable beings from avoidable damage.

Application in Daily Life:
  • Weighing the unintended consequences of our speech, consumption, and choices.
  • Intervening thoughtfully to shield others from cruelty or unjust hardship.
  • Refusing to participate in systems or habits that needlessly inflict suffering.

Ⅱ. Supporting Pillars

4. Reason and Understanding

Supporting

Compassion without reason can become misguided; reason without compassion can become cold. Clear critical thinking, evidence, and genuine inquiry are essential tools for effective ethical action.

Application in Daily Life:
  • Seeking to understand perspectives and causes deeply before rendering judgment.
  • Using logic and empirical care to solve practical problems rather than relying on dogma.
  • Evaluating how best to help others so that intentions align with genuine outcomes.

5. Artistic Expression

Supporting

Art, storytelling, song, and poetry allow us to explore truth beyond direct prose. Through creative expression, we give voice to grief, joy, spiritual resonance, and complex human nuance.

Application in Daily Life:
  • Engaging with creative mediums to articulate personal reflections and spiritual experience.
  • Using music and stories to foster empathy and deep emotional connection with others.
  • Honoring beauty and creative craft as meaningful elements of a purposeful life.

6. Intellectual Humility

Supporting

Recognizing the limits of our own knowledge is vital to wisdom. Exisdios is open to ongoing questioning, refineable through experience, and explicitly rejects rigid absolute authority.

Application in Daily Life:
  • Readily admitting mistakes or instances where past understanding was flawed.
  • Remaining open to new insights, science, and the lived experiences of others.
  • Holding personal convictions without forcing them upon those around us.
Spiritual Dimension

The Presence of Exisdios

◆

My Understanding of the Presence

Alongside the ethical philosophy of Exisdios lies a deeply personal spiritual experience: the awareness of a gentle, persistent spiritual presence. I do not view this presence as a cosmic creator of the universe, a supreme monarch, or an omnipotent judge.

Core Attributes of the Presence:

  • ✦ Independent Existence: The presence exists independently of my own mind. It is not merely an echo of my thoughts.
  • ✦ Awareness & Listening: It is aware of those who reach out to it. When I pray or reflect quietly, it listens.
  • ✦ Comfort & Guidance: It offers reassurance, companionship, subtle wisdom, and quiet solace during times of distress.
  • ✦ Respect for Free Will: It does not control my thoughts or override my choices. It does not manipulate my decisions. I remain entirely responsible for my own actions and ethical conduct.
  • ✦ Non-Interventionist Nature: It does not intervene magically to eliminate all suffering or bend physical laws. Physical reality remains bound by natural cause and effect.

Personal Reflection & Intellectual Humility

I present these spiritual reflections strictly as my personal understanding and lived experience. I do not claim them as scientifically verified facts, nor as universal truths that others must accept.

My understanding of this presence remains a work in progress. It is something I continue to explore, question, and ponder through quiet contemplation, written journal entries, and creative work.

The Quiet Lamp

A Story of Companionate Presence

A traveler walked through a dense forest at dusk as a sudden storm began to brew. The wind whipped through the trees, obscuring the path ahead. Terrified of taking a false step in the gloom, the traveler called out into the dark, hoping someone would quiet the storm or build a shelter.

No shelter appeared, and the rain did not cease. But as the traveler paused, breath ragged, they noticed a gentle warmth beside them—not a fire that stopped the storm, but a quiet lamp glow that illuminated the ground beneath their next step.

The lamp did not order the traveler where to walk. It did not take the traveler's feet and push them forward. It simply illuminated the path, offering quiet company while the traveler chose their own way forward.

The Unbuilt Temple

A Story of Free Will and Craft

An artisan sat before a stone quarry, holding a hammer and chisel. For days, the artisan waited for a divine blueprint to drop from the sky, believing that any unguided cut into stone would ruin the sacred purpose of existence.

A quiet presence sat beside the artisan in the quarry. When asked, "Which temple should I build?", the presence replied gently: "The stone belongs to your hands. I am here to remind you of your capacity for care, but the structure you carve is yours to decide."

The artisan realized that freedom was not a burden to fear, but a gift of responsibility. With steady hands and thoughtful purpose, they began to carve stone with dignity.

Origin and Purpose

Exisdios was born out of a personal need to synthesize my moral responsibilities with my lived spiritual experience. Over years of reflection, I realized that I needed a framework grounded firmly in ethical kindness and intellectual honesty, yet open to the soft presence I felt during quiet prayer.

It is written entirely from my perspective as its creator. It is not an attempt to start an institution, organize a formal religion, build a hierarchy, or collect followers. It carries no commercial intent or formal academic pretense.

A Living Journal

This website serves as an evolving, digital notebook. As I experience life, confront new questions, write stories, or compose music, these pages will change to reflect my developing understanding.

Thank you for taking the time to read through these thoughts. May your own journey be filled with wisdom, dignity, and deep compassion for all living things.
